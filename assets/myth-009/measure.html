MyTh 009 measurement

This page reproduces the token counts and vocabulary comparison in MyTh 009, The Vocabulary Budget. It downloads six public tokenizers from Hugging Face, tokenizes Article 1 of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights in 26 languages, and compares each vocabulary with Gemma 3's.
