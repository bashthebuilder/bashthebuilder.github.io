Student Voice

Unsolicited, and not always flattering.

These arrived by email, unprompted, from students who had nothing to gain. The critical ones are here too — the point of feedback is that you act on it.

Afternoon mrs, I would just like to put in a small word of gratitude & say today’s lecture felt very engaging… considering it was his first time lecturing here, he engaged well I believe — he ensured we understood what was briefed in the powerpoints and just made things easier… I just felt more engaged than usual today. — A student, emailing his own tutor after my first-ever lecture at Kent (CO639)
Congratulations Shoaib! This feedback is from a student who never ever contacts me usually (and he’s my tutee!) and has not really been engaging with several courses. We need to observe your teaching rather than the other way around, it seems :) — The tutor who received that email, forwarding it on
I really enjoy attending your lecture and prefer this approach to teaching, however, I feel sometimes it can go off-topic. I enjoy sharing my opinion and having the class getting more involved gives the lecture a better atmosphere. — CO334, People and Computing — the criticism I acted on
You seem more charismatic than almost all of the other lecturers and I think the way you teach is engaging too. The only feedback i can give is not about you but is infact about the module being a little basic. — CO334, People and Computing
I really like your style of teaching. The idea of a discussion first gets you thinking and the slides afterwards cement it in your mind. The only negative I would say is you ask too much if your teaching style is okay. — A mature student, CO639 — fair, and I stopped asking
Just wanted to say I loved the lecture today! And your methods have helped us a lot. Thank you! — Final-year student, Semantic Web (CO644), 2019
Two students told me the same thing in different words: discuss first, then show the slides. That one piece of student feedback reshaped how I teach every module since.
