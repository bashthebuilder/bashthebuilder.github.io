Research Vision & Impact

Bridging Science and Scale.

My research lives at the intersection of Probabilistic Graphical Models and Deep Learning. I focus on making AI more robust, interpretable, and culturally aware, ensuring that the theoretical breakthroughs we achieve in the lab can survive the complexities of real-world deployment.

Industrial Impact

From intent classification at British Telecom to multi-modal recommendation engines at eBay, my research is operational within Tier-1 global infrastructures, solving problems at a scale few academic models ever reach.

Global Recognition

Honored as a finalist for the Best Research Awards in Hong Kong and recipient of the ACS Digital Disruptor Award. My work has been featured on Forbes and BBC Radio 4.
