Featured Project

PICASO

Probabilistic Conceptual Spaces Sensemaking under Uncertainty

Funded by the Alan Turing Institute’s Defence & Security Programme, PICASO revolutionizes intelligence analysis by making uncertainty a "first-class citizen." Our PROCS Engine replaces brittle, static knowledge graphs with probabilistic clouds, enabling systems to mirror human cognitive reasoning and provide "honest confidence" in high-stakes decision-making.
